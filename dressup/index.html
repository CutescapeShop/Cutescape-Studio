Dress-up / Paper Doll — Base Doll Prototype

Isolated prototype. Does not affect Name Keychain, Letter Beads, or Clicker.

First prototype: base doll silhouette only (no hair, clothing, or accessories). Chibi proportions, 135 mm target height, flat 2.5D body with simple raised printed eyes and mouth (no eye sockets or cabochon attachment system yet — a separate glass-eye version will be tested later).

Exports two STL files: the base body silhouette and the shallow raised face details, so the face can be printed as a second color.
